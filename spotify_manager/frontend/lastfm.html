02 / FAMILIAR PATTERNS

Musical eras

Similarity compares every artist's share of recorded plays: sum of minimum shares ÷ sum of maximum shares (weighted Jaccard). 100% means the same artist mix; 0% means no shared artists. Empty periods are not comparable. This describes recorded music, without inferring life events.
