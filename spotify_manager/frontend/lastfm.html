02 / FAMILIAR PATTERNS

Musical eras

Similarity compares every artist's share of recorded plays: sum of minimum shares ÷ sum of maximum shares (weighted Jaccard). 100% means the same artist mix; 0% means no shared artists. Empty periods are not comparable. This describes recorded music, without inferring life events.

CURRENT LABELS / HISTORICAL PLAYS

Community tag atlas

Current community tags are applied to your selected historical listening. Tags may describe genres, eras or other community labels; they do not establish an artist’s historical genre. Every play stays visible, including Unknown / no tags.

Tags powered by Last.fm. Loading this view uses cached metadata only. Explicit lookups send artist names, not scrobble records or account history, to Last.fm.

Top eight tag labels remain fixed across the selection; remaining labels form Other tags. Three-tag mode divides each play equally among available leading tags, so allocated values can be fractional. Leading-tag mode assigns each play once. Select a colored area or use Artist evidence to inspect supporting artists and their recorded history.

ARTISTS / REPORTED PLACES

Artist atlas

Explore countries associated with artists in your selected listening. Current MusicBrainz metadata is applied to historical plays. A country association is not necessarily birthplace or formation origin, nationality, or where you listened. Multiple candidates and countries stay explicit.

Artist metadata from MusicBrainz. Loading this view uses cached metadata only. Explicit lookups send artist names, not your scrobble records or account history, to MusicBrainz.

Country totals use only one exact-name candidate with one reported country. All other plays remain in the evidence table. Select a country to inspect supporting artists, or pan and zoom the map. Small or historical countries may appear only in the complete country table. Outlines: Natural Earth · Source notes.

DATED / CANDIDATE EVIDENCE

Expand country coverage

Fetch up to 25 highest-play artists in this selection whose country metadata is missing or at least 30 days old. Requests use artist names to find country evidence on MusicBrainz.

Load all missing artists to search your full history, regardless of the current dates and filters. MusicBrainz allows roughly one request per second, so the first pass can take several hours. The Space saves completed lookups as it goes. Run the loader again to continue after an interruption.

CURRENT / REPRESENTATIVE ARTWORK

Bring the covers into view

Opening this view reuses cached covers and sends the titles and artist credits of visible albums without artwork to Last.fm. Use Fetch to search for remaining artwork through MusicBrainz and get public covers from the Cover Art Archive and Internet Archive. No play counts, scrobble records or account identifiers are sent. These are current representative covers, rather than historical artwork.

Recent observations are reused for 30 days. Country and cover lookups share MusicBrainz’s request limit. Completed observations and images survive cancellation; the separate 256-album cache evicts its oldest entries and can reset after a Space rebuild. Unavailable images stay as placeholders, including in exports.

09 / BETWEEN THE RECORDS

Listening sessions

Group consecutive selected records by the gap between their timestamps. A gap strictly greater than your threshold starts a new session; equal gaps stay together. These are inferred groups, not measured continuous listening.

Only records matching the date, artist and rhythm filters are grouped. Filters can split activity or cut its edges. Midnight and daylight-saving changes do not split a group by themselves. The first and last selected groups may continue outside this selection.

Session starts over time

Every matching group belongs to the local week or month of its first selected record, including groups crossing midnight. Charts cover all matching groups, independently of the list page.
